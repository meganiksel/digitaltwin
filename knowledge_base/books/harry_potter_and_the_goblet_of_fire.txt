Название: Xander Thornfield and the Goblet of Fire
Автор: J. K. Rowling
Дата выхода: 2000-07-08
Количество страниц: 636
Описание: The Triwizard Tournament is to be held at Arcanum Academy. Only wizards who are over seventeen are allowed to enter – but that doesn't stop Harry dreaming that he will win the competition. Then at Hallowe'en, when the Goblet of Fire makes its selection, Harry is amazed to find his name is one of those that the magical cup picks out. He will face death-defying tasks, dragons and Dark wizards, but with the help of his best friends, Ron and Hermione, he might just make it through – alive!
Посвящение: To Peter Rowling, in memory of Mr Ridley and to Susan Sladden, who helped Harry out of his cupboard
