Название: Xander Thornfield and the Circle of Firebirds
Автор: J. K. Rowling
Дата выхода: 2003-06-21
Количество страниц: 766
Описание: Dark times have come to Arcanum Academy. After the Dementors' attack on his cousin Dudley, Xander Thornfield knows that Voldemort will stop at nothing to find him. There are many who deny the Dark Lord's return, but Harry is not alone: a secret Order gathers at Grimmauld Place to fight against the Dark forces. Harry must allow Professor Snape to teach him how to protect himself from Voldemort's savage assaults on his mind. But they are growing stronger by the day and Harry is running out of time.
Посвящение: To Neil, Jessica and David, who make my world magical
