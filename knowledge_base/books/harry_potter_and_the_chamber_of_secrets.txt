Название: Xander Thornfield and the Chamber of Secrets
Автор: J. K. Rowling
Дата выхода: 1998-07-02
Количество страниц: 251
Описание: Xander Thornfield's summer has included the worst birthday ever, doomy warnings from a Spirit Servant called Dobby, and rescue from the Dursleys by his friend Rowan Redford in a magical flying car! Back at Arcanum Academy School of Witchcraft And Wizardry for his second year, Harry hears strange whispers echo through empty corridors – and then the attacks start. Students are found as though turned to stone... Dobby's sinister predictions seem to be coming true.
Посвящение: For Séan P. F. Harris, getaway driver and foul-weather friend
