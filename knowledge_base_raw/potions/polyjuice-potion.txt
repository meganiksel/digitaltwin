Название: Polyjuice Potion
Эффект: Allowed a human drinker to temporarily assume the form of another person
Побочные эффекты: Attempts to transform into animals or part-humans would not reverse automatically
Характеристики: Thick and mud-like, Bubbles slowly, Taste and colour varied depending on the person being turned into
Время приготовления: One month
Сложность: Advanced
