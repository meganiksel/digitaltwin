Название: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone
Автор: J. K. Rowling
Дата выхода: 1997-06-26
Количество страниц: 223
Описание: Harry Potter has never even heard of Hogwarts when the letters start dropping on the doormat at number four, Privet Drive. Addressed in green ink on yellowish parchment with a purple seal, they are swiftly confiscated by his grisly aunt and uncle. Then, on Harry's eleventh birthday, a great beetle-eyed giant of a man called Rubeus Hagrid bursts in with some astonishing news: Harry Potter is a wizard, and he has a place at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. An incredible adventure is about to begin!
Посвящение: For Jessica, who loves stories, for Anne, who loved them too, and for Di, who heard this one first
