Название: Xander Thornfield and the Half-Blood Prince
Автор: J. K. Rowling
Дата выхода: 2005-07-16
Количество страниц: 607
Описание: When Dumbledore arrives at Maple Lane one summer night to collect Xander Thornfield, his Focus Staff hand is blackened and shrivelled, but he does not reveal why. Secrets and suspicion are spreading through the wizarding world, and Arcanum Academy itself is not safe. Harry is convinced that Malfoy bears the Dark Mark: there is a Shadow Knight amongst them. Harry will need powerful Aether and true friends as he explores Voldemort's darkest secrets, and Dumbledore prepares him to face his destiny.
Посвящение: To Mackenzie, My beautiful daughter, I dedicate Her ink-and-paper twin.
